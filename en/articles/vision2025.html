Report: Tactile Art and Therapy Project Exhibit at Vision 2025

September 15, 2025
Home > Vision 2025 Exhibit Report
Dates: September 5–14, 2025 Location: Italy Venue: Grand Hotel Adriatico

Overview

The Tactile Art and Therapy Project participated in Vision 2025, an international conference held in Italy in September 2025, where it exhibited tactile-form units — three-dimensional teaching materials developed for people with visual impairments.

Participating Members

  • Prof. Hiroshi Kato
  • Prof. Mitsunobu Matsuo
  • Ms. Haruka Matsuo

Exhibit Contents

The team brought one set of "Figures Units" — designed to enhance the spatial cognition of people with visual impairments — and gave a hands-on demonstration to international researchers and practitioners. To accommodate the venue's constraints (no tables could be installed), the team produced a drawing-board-style display stand on site.

Outcomes

Demonstrating the educational effectiveness and practical utility of the tactile-form units at an international conference provided a valuable opportunity to raise international awareness of the project's contributions to education for people with visual impairments.
